# Kestrel DC-60 field guide (excerpt)

- LOCK_TIMEOUT: connector lock did not reach position. Try UnlockConnector; replace the lock actuator if it recurs.
- PM_OVERTEMP: power module over temperature, charging limited. Replace the power module; resets only delay the trip.
- GROUND_FAULT: residual current / insulation fault. Do not reset remotely. Inspect and replace the cable.
- PT-OFFLINE: payment terminal offline. Reset the station.
- Short sessions that end with an EV communication error on one connector usually mean a worn cable (part: cable).
